# AMB BOUTIQUE creative direction

All current editorial photographs were generated with the built-in image-generation model and art-directed for a polished San Diego women’s boutique. They are launch-stage visual assets, not representations of inventory. Product-specific imagery will replace catalog placeholders after the real CSV and product photographs arrive.

## Visual system

- Warm ivory, sand, camel, soft rose and espresso palette
- Natural coastal daylight with restrained editorial contrast
- Contemporary feminine styling for the US, Canada, UK, Australia and New Zealand
- Clean negative space for responsive copy placement
- No text, logos, watermarks or recognisable third-party branding inside the generated images

## Prompt set

### Four homepage hero scenes

`Luxury women’s fashion editorial for a San Diego boutique, warm cream architectural setting and soft coastal daylight, diverse adult fashion models in refined dresses and polished separates, elegant natural movement, premium campaign photography, ample negative space for ecommerce headline and CTA, timeless neutral palette with camel and soft rose accents, no text, logo or watermark, wide cinematic composition.`

Individual scenes varied between a three-model collection portrait, a white coastal dress, a terracotta blouse with ivory trousers, and a black dress styled with a structured cream handbag.

### Collection category editorials

`Premium ecommerce fashion contact sheet with four equal clean editorial frames, adult women shown head-to-toe on a consistent warm pale-beige studio background, refined contemporary styling covering dresses, tops, playsuits, skirts, shorts, knitwear, handbags and heels, crop-safe margins, no text, logos or watermarks.`

### Narrow collection banner

`Ultra-wide women’s boutique editorial banner, coordinated group in neutral and soft terracotta looks, warm limestone architecture, subject placement leaves calm negative space for a centered collection title, sophisticated natural daylight, no text or logo.`

### Narrow product-page banner

`Ultra-wide luxury fashion campaign detail for a women’s boutique, model in a refined dark dress near sculptural tropical foliage and warm plaster, subject offset to preserve copy space, elegant low-contrast light, no text or logo.`

### Journal images

`Editorial lifestyle photograph for a San Diego women’s fashion journal, sophisticated adult women, premium magazine realism, warm coastal palette, natural gestures, clean styling and composition suitable for an ecommerce article card, no text, logo or watermark.`

The three journal directions were brunch-to-golden-hour dressing, the everyday handbag edit and a five-piece capsule wardrobe.
